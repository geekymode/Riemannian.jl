References

Books

  • H. M. Edwards, Riemann's Zeta Function, Academic Press, 1974 (Dover reprint 2001). Includes a translation of Riemann's paper.
  • E. C. Titchmarsh, The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function, 2nd ed., revised by D. R. Heath-Brown, Oxford, 1986.
  • H. Iwaniec, E. Kowalski, Analytic Number Theory, AMS Colloquium Publications 53, 2004.
  • P. Borwein, S. Choi, B. Rooney, A. Weirathmueller (eds.), The Riemann Hypothesis: A Resource for the Afficionado and Virtuoso Alike, CMS Books in Mathematics, Springer, 2008.
  • B. Mazur, W. Stein, Prime Numbers and the Riemann Hypothesis, Cambridge, 2016. Very visual and elementary.
  • H. Davenport, Multiplicative Number Theory, 3rd ed., Springer GTM 74, 2000.

Original and landmark papers

  • B. Riemann, Ueber die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse, Monatsber. Berlin Akad., 1859.
  • G. H. Hardy, Sur les zéros de la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 158 (1914).
  • A. Selberg, On the zeros of Riemann's zeta-function, Skr. Norske Vid. Akad. Oslo (1942).
  • H. L. Montgomery, The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 24 (1973).
  • N. Levinson, More than one third of zeros of Riemann's zeta-function are on σ = 1/2, Adv. Math. 13 (1974).
  • C. M. Newman, Fourier transforms with only real zeros, Proc. AMS 61 (1976).
  • G. Robin, Grandes valeurs de la fonction somme des diviseurs et hypothèse de Riemann, J. Math. Pures Appl. 63 (1984).
  • A. M. Odlyzko, H. J. J. te Riele, Disproof of the Mertens conjecture, J. reine angew. Math. 357 (1985).
  • A. M. Odlyzko, On the distribution of spacings between zeros of the zeta function, Math. Comp. 48 (1987).
  • J. B. Conrey, More than two fifths of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are on the critical line, J. reine angew. Math. 399 (1989).
  • X.-J. Li, The positivity of a sequence of numbers and the Riemann hypothesis, J. Number Theory 65 (1997).
  • J. C. Lagarias, An elementary problem equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis, Amer. Math. Monthly 109 (2002).
  • P. Borwein, An efficient algorithm for the Riemann zeta function, CMS Conf. Proc. 27 (2000).

Recent results

  • D. H. J. Polymath, Effective approximation of heat flow evolution of the Riemann ξ function, and a new upper bound for the de Bruijn–Newman constant, Res. Math. Sci. 6 (2019).
  • B. Rodgers, T. Tao, The de Bruijn–Newman constant is non-negative, Forum Math. Pi 8 (2020).
  • K. Pratt, N. Robles, A. Zaharescu, D. Zeindler, More than five-twelfths of the zeros of ζ are on the critical line, Res. Math. Sci. 7 (2020).
  • D. J. Platt, T. S. Trudgian, The Riemann hypothesis is true up to 3·10¹², Bull. LMS 53 (2021).
  • L. Guth, J. Maynard, New large value estimates for Dirichlet polynomials, arXiv:2405.20552 (2024).

Online

  • A. Odlyzko's tables of zeros: https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/zeta_tables/
  • LMFDB, the L-functions and Modular Forms Database: https://www.lmfdb.org
  • Clay Mathematics Institute problem description (E. Bombieri): https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/
